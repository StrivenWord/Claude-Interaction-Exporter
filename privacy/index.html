    Privacy

    On this page

    The extension has no backend. There is no account to create, no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party service of any kind. The only server it contacts is Claude.ai itself, to retrieve your own data; everything it reads is written to a file on your own computer.

    The full privacy policy — what the extension handles, what it stores, and the permissions it requests — will be published here.
