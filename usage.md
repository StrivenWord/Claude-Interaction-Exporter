## Export the current conversation

1. Open a conversation or a Cowork session in Claude.ai.
2. Click the extension icon.
3. Choose a format, and optionally fill in project, contributor, and tags.
4. Click **Export Current Conversation**.

## Browse and bulk export

Click the extension icon → **Browse All Conversations** to search, filter, sort, and export in bulk as a ZIP.

## Output formats

| Format | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Markdown | Human-readable, with YAML frontmatter |
| Plain text | Simple transcript |
| JSON | Complete raw data, every branch |
